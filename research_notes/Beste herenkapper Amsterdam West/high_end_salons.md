# High-end / premium men's hair salons in Amsterdam (for a modern new look, possibly a perm later), as of Nov 2026

> Method note: the session could only use WebSearch. Every WebFetch failed (fresha.com, keune.com, man-man.nl, haarbarbaar.nl did not resolve in this sandbox). ## Q1. Which premium salons / stylists exist, where are they, and are they open in 2026?

### Takeaway
The confirmed candidates in or near the target cycling area are: **Menspire** (5 Amsterdam branches including Old West at Bilderdijkstraat 198 and De Pijp; a premium barber/men's-grooming chain), **Haarbarbaar** (Rosmarijnsteeg 4, Centrum, near the Negen Straatjes and Spui), **Rob Peetoom** (Elandsgracht 68, Jordaan, plus Zuidas; a full salon with stylist tiers), **Kinki Kappers** (a chain with branches on Overtoom and Admiraal de Ruijterweg in West), and **The Building AMS** (Stadionweg 184, Zuid; named by Vogue NL for 2026). Herman & Richard van Bolderick (Pieter Baststraat 2a, De Pijp/Zuid border) is a classic appointment-only men's barber rather than a "next level" style salon. Several names from the brief could not be verified at all.

## Q2. Price for a men's cut (senior/top vs junior), and is consultation included?

### Takeaway
Premium men's cuts in Amsterdam run roughly **€45–€65**. Senior or top-stylist tiers at full salons reach about €60–€80, especially for longer hair. Menspire and Rob Peetoom both price by stylist level. None of the snippets says whether a separate style consultation is included. The source for each figure is listed below.

### Cited Findings
- **Menspire**:
  - Old West on Fresha: services from €34 (30–45 min, "up to 15% off") and a 45-min service from €46.75. Another language version lists "HAIRCUT & STYLE" 30 min from €40.
  - Range across branches about €25–65 (Old West) and €25–70 (De Pijp), with haircut-and-style entries from about €47.50. Prices differ between snapshots.
  - Fresha names senior stylists: Andrea at Old West, Francesco at De Pijp.
  - Treatwell says Menspire currently does not accept Treatwell bookings.
  - Sources: [Fresha Old West](https://www.fresha.com/a/menspire-old-west-amsterdam-bilderdijkstraat-198-p2ozsxmk); [Fresha De Pijp](https://www.fresha.com/a/menspire-de-pijp-amsterdam-gerard-douplein-12-dtp7uewu); [Treatwell Menspire](https://www.treatwell.nl/en/place/menspire/)
- **Haarbarbaar**: cut €50, beard trim €20, combo €65. One site shows "from €35", possibly a lower-tier service (unclear). — [trendheads.nl](https://trendheads.nl/kapper/haarbarbaar/); [top-rated.online](https://www.top-rated.online/cities/Amsterdam/place/p/13943956/HaarBarbaar)

## Q3. Do they do men's perms or texture treatments?

### Takeaway
Men's perms are available in Amsterdam-West, typically **€65–€100** for 1–1.5 hours. They were confirmed on booking platforms for Fairfax (Kinkerstraat) and a few non-premium salons. Specialist "digital perm" expertise was claimed by Hair Art Amsterdam (Centrum). No source confirmed perms at Menspire, Haarbarbaar, Rob Peetoom, Kinki or The Building.

### Cited Findings
- On Treatwell Amsterdam-West, one listing shows a men's perm "vanaf €72,25" for 1h–1h30. Barbershop Alles in bedrijf (Nieuw-West) offers men's perms from €65 (1.5h). Blue Dreams Curly Hair (Steigereiland) starts from €100. — [Treatwell Permanent Amsterdam-West](https://www.treatwell.nl/salons/behandeling-permanent/aanbod-type-lokaal/in-amsterdam-west-nl/); [Treatwell Permanent Amsterdam](https://www.treatwell.nl/salons/behandeling-permanent/aanbod-type-lokaal/in-amsterdam-nl/)
- Hair & Beauty Sen (Bos en Lommerweg) lists men's perms. Hair Art Amsterdam (Centrum) describes itself as an "absolute Expert Professional for Digital Perm". — [Hair Art Amsterdam](https://hairartamsterdam.nl/en/home-2/); [Treatwell](https://www.treatwell.nl/salons/behandeling-permanent/aanbod-type-lokaal/in-amsterdam-west-nl/)
- Fairfax Amsterdam (West) appears on Fresha's Amsterdam perm listing page. — [Fresha perms](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/tt/hair-perms/in/nl-amsterdam)
- B Academy Amsterdam runs a one-day "Men Perm" course for stylists. — [B Academy](https://hair.bacademy.nl/cursussen/men-perm)
- HOLO Salon's guide covers the textured crop. BRUSH lists a "textured fringe cut". Neither ties these styles to a perm. — [HOLO](https://www.holo.salon/blog/guide-to-mens-haircuts-in-amsterdam); [BRUSH](https://brushhm.com/pages/men)

### Inferences
- A practical route is to get the cut and new shape at a premium cutter, then do the perm later at a salon that lists men's perms. Fairfax is a candidate because it does both senior long-hair cuts and perms, in Oud-West. A Hair Art-type digital-perm specialist is another option. Ask for a consult with reference photos first.

### Gaps
- No confirmation in any source of perm availability at Menspire, Haarbarbaar, Rob Peetoom, Kinki or The Building. Ask them directly.

## Q5. What makes them premium, and how do you book (lead time)?

### Takeaway
Premium signals found:
- **Rob Peetoom**: a long-standing Dutch top salon brand with a stylist-tier system.
- **The Building**: on the Vogue NL 2026 list and a Keune partner.
- **Menspire**: scale, a men-only grooming concept and named senior stylists.
- **Haarbarbaar**: cited on "best herenkapper" lists.

Booking is online for nearly all of them: Fresha for Menspire, Treatwell for Haarbarbaar, the Kinki website, and robpeetoom.com. Rob Peetoom and Van Bolderick are appointment-only. No source gave lead times.

### Cited Findings
- Rob Peetoom: appointment only. Tiered stylist pricing (junior, stylist, senior, top). — [Yelp](https://m.yelp.com/biz/rob-peetoom-amsterdam-3?hrid=srGQWqPslN81y7Wr4SUUdw); [wiewathaar PDF](https://www.wiewathaar.nl/images/hairdressers/25.pdf)
- The Building AMS: named on Vogue NL's 2026 favourites list, works with Keune, and Keune calls it "very chic". — [Vogue NL](https://www.vogue.nl/beauty/kappers-amsterdam/); [Keune](https://www.keune.com/nl/beste-kappers-amsterdam/)
- Menspire: Fresha booking for every branch, with named senior stylists. Not bookable via Treatwell. — [Fresha](https://www.fresha.com/a/menspire-old-west-amsterdam-bilderdijkstraat-198-p2ozsxmk); [Treatwell](https://www.treatwell.nl/en/place/menspire/)

- HIS Amsterdam: "no standard cut", tailored styling, since 2007 (self-description). — [hisamsterdam.nl](http://www.hisamsterdam.nl/herenkapper-amsterdam/)

### Inferences
- For "next level" styling advice and longer or textured cuts, the strongest fits found are:
  - Rob Peetoom Elandsgracht (top-stylist tier, Jordaan);
  - Fairfax Kinkerstraat (senior long-hair cut, perms, nearest to West);
  - a senior stylist at Menspire Old West (convenient, very consistent reviews, but barber-format slots);
  - Haarbarbaar (craft, honest advice, €50).
- The Building AMS is premium but far away (Zuid/Stadionweg).

### Gaps

- No booking lead times found. Check live availability on Fresha or Treatwell.
