# Men's Perm Specialists in Amsterdam (K-perm, Japanese digital perm, textured or wave perm), as of Nov 2026

**Method note (applies to everything below):** In this session, direct page fetches were blocked by the network sandbox (september-hair.com and fresha.com both failed DNS resolution, so treatwell, instagram, google and others were not attempted). All findings therefore come from **web-search result snippets and summaries of the cited pages**, not from full page reads. Prices, ratings and review counts are snapshots from those pages at index time. Some figures conflict between snapshots, and the conflicts are flagged. **No Google Maps rating could be verified directly for any salon**, and **no Reddit thread about men's perms in Amsterdam was found**. Because everything comes from indexed listings, "still open in 2026" means "has a current-looking listing", not "confirmed by phone".

## 1. Which salons exist, where are they, and are they operating in 2026?

### Takeaway
The strongest real men's perm options are the Japanese-run salons: SEPTEMBER (Zuid/Buitenveldert), Atmosphere Studio (Jordaan), MUSUBI Lab. (Oud-West), Hair Studio Picnic (Jordaan) and ASSORT (Centrum). There is also one Korean-style specialist, JT Hair Design, a private salon in Nieuw-West. ### Inferences
- For a "next level" men's textured or K-perm with cut, budget about **€150–€160** for a cold/Korean perm or **€170–€220** for a digital perm, and expect **2.5–3.5 hours** in the chair.
- A digital (heat) perm suits mid-length hair that should look like a soft, loose, blow-dried wave. A cold/Korean perm on shorter men's hair (about 5–10 cm on top) is what most "textured/K-perm" photos show. This is general hair knowledge consistent with Picnic's description of digital perms working mid-lengths to ends, not a salon quote.

## 5. Booking, consultation, and suitability warnings

### Takeaway
Nearly all candidates book online through Fresha (SEPTEMBER, FRAMED, MUSUBI, Atmosphere, JT) or Treatwell (Masters of Hair, Golden Hand, Zunara, Hair Art). JT Hair Design is easiest to reach via WhatsApp or KakaoTalk. No salon was confirmed to offer a free perm consultation. The key suitability warning found is that **digital perms cannot be done on bleached hair** (Atmosphere).

### Cited Findings
- Atmosphere Studio books via Booking.page and Addagio as well as Fresha. — [Booking.page](https://booking.page/en/company/page/atmospherestudio); [Addagio](https://addagio.io/book/atmosphere-studio-japanese-hair-salon-amsterdam)
- Atmosphere Studio: digital perms "are suitable for Asian hair. It cannot be done on bleached hair." — [atmospherestudio-amsterdam.com](https://atmospherestudio-amsterdam.com/v2/)
- Picnic: digital perm wave sits from the mid-lengths to the ends, so it needs length. — [hairstudio-picnic.com](https://hairstudio-picnic.com/)
- JT Hair Design: WhatsApp/phone +31 6 58822079 and KakaoTalk. Mon–Sat 10:00–17:30. — [Facebook](https://web.facebook.com/JThairstylist/); [Fresha](https://www.fresha.com/lvp/jt-hair-design-amsterdam-asian-hair-p-hans-frankfurthersingel-amsterdam-a2NlBE)
- Fresha lists a "Hair Consultations" category for Amsterdam, but no specific perm salon was confirmed to offer a free consultation. — [Fresha consultations](https://www.fresha.com/lp/en/tt/salon-hair-consultations/in/nl-amsterdam)

### Inferences
- Atmosphere's note suggests digital perms may be calibrated for Asian hair. A European client with fine hair should ask whether a cold/Korean perm is the better choice. Coloured or bleached hair should be disclosed up front.
- A practical approach is to DM or WhatsApp two or three shortlisted salons (e.g., MUSUBI, Atmosphere, JT, SEPTEMBER) with photos of current hair and target look, before booking a 3-hour slot.

### Gaps
- Waiting times and lead time for appointments were not visible.
- No salon-specific aftercare instructions were found (e.g., no wash for 48 h, curl cream). This is general knowledge only and should be asked at the salon.
